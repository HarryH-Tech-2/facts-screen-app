# Privacy Policy — Lock Screen Facts

**Effective date:** August 18, 2026

Lock Screen Facts ("the app") is developed by Harry Harrison. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. The short version: **the app collects no personal data, and nothing you do in the app ever leaves your device.**

## Information we collect

**None.** The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. It does not transmit any information about you or your device to us or to any third party.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

- **Your settings** — enabled fact categories, refresh interval, theme choice, and wallpaper preferences. These are saved in the app's private storage.
- **Your background photo (optional)** — if you choose a photo as your lock-screen background, a copy is saved in the app's private storage so the wallpaper can be refreshed in the background. The app can only access the specific photo you pick; it never browses or uploads your photo library.
- **The fact queue** — a list of facts scheduled to appear on your lock screen.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

## Permissions the app uses

- **Set wallpaper** — used to place the current fact on your lock screen. Wallpaper images are generated on your device.
- **Notifications** — used to show facts as local notifications on the schedule you choose. Notifications are created on your device; there is no push-notification server.
- **Photos (optional)** — used only when you choose a photo as your wallpaper background, via the system photo picker.

## Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled (for example, if in-app purchases are added), this policy will be updated here before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
