# Play Store Listing — Lock Screen Facts

## App name (30 chars max)

Lock Screen Facts

## Short description (80 chars max — this one is 68)

Learn something new every time you unlock. Facts on your lock screen.

## Long description (4000 chars max)

Every time you pick up your phone is a chance to learn something. Lock Screen Facts puts a fascinating fact right on your lock screen and refreshes it on a schedule you choose — so a quick glance at the clock becomes a tiny bit of knowledge, dozens of times a day.

**How it works**

Pick the topics you love, choose how often you want a new fact, and that's it. The app renders each fact as a beautiful wallpaper and sets it on your lock screen automatically — even when the app is closed.

**Features**

• Six categories to mix and match: Science, History, Space, Animals, Geography, and Human Body
• A new fact every 15 minutes, 30 minutes, hour, or once a day — you decide
• Facts appear as your lock-screen wallpaper, refreshed automatically in the background
• Use the built-in themed background, or choose one of your own photos
• Optional notifications deliver facts on the same schedule
• A playful, hand-made design with light and dark modes
• Browse the full fact collection any time inside the app

**Private by design**

No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and no data collection. Everything — your settings, your photo, the wallpapers themselves — is created and stored on your device and never leaves it.

**Works offline**

The entire fact collection lives in the app. Airplane mode, no signal, doesn't matter — your lock screen keeps teaching.

Turn the habit of checking your phone into the habit of learning something new. Install Lock Screen Facts and make every unlock count.
